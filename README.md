# Shima 

Shima is a language learning app built around a **Natural Language Learning (NLL) first approach**, inspired by the **Language Islands** method developed by **Mikel Telleria**.

The idea behind Shima is based on the way humans naturally acquire languages: through meaningful exposure, active recall, and repeated interaction with language that is slightly beyond their current ability.

Learning happens most effectively when the brain is challenged. Instead of only consuming information passively, learners improve by struggling to understand, recall, and produce language. That process of attempting, making mistakes, and correcting yourself helps create stronger connections and deeper understanding.

Using the Language Islands concept, Shima organizes learning into focused areas of communication. Each island represents a specific situation or topic, containing useful sentences, phrases, and conversations that learners can gradually master.

Rather than memorizing isolated words or studying grammar separately, learners build their language ability through complete language chunks and real-world examples. As more islands are developed, they begin to connect with each other, creating a wider understanding of the language and allowing learners to communicate across more situations.

Each island focuses on practical language use, such as:

* Introductions
* Daily conversations
* Travel
* Food and restaurants
* Hobbies
* Work and study
* Social interactions
* Expressing opinions
* Advanced conversations

The structure and learning philosophy of Shima are inspired by Mikel Telleria's **Language Islands** method and **Natural Language Learning (NLL)** approach.
